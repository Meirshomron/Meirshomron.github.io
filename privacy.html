Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026
Welcome to our game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our mobile application. We care deeply about your privacy, especially when it comes to children. Because our game is designed for players of all ages, including children under the age of 13, we follow strict privacy rules to keep young players safe.

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We fully comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal information from children under the age of 13. We do not ask users to enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identification.

2. Information We Collect

Our game is built to respect your privacy. Personal Data: We do not collect any personal data (such as your real name, address, or contact details). Gameplay Data: The game may save high scores, unlocked levels, or game settings locally on your device. This data stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep our game free to play, we may display advertisements or use basic diagnostic tools. Safe for Kids: Any third-party advertising networks or analytics tools used in this game are certified Google Play Families Ads Providers. No Tracking: These services are configured to run in a strict, non-personalized mode for children. They do not track your behavior across other apps or build behavioral profiles. They only serve contextual ads appropriate for families.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page and updated with a new date at the top.

5. Contact Us

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at our developer email address provided on our Google Play Store page.
